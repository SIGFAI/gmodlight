# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public version.

- Garry's Mod hands in Dying Light (F2): physgun, weapons, weapon wheel, HUD,
  with Kyle's own arms, weapon and weapon HUD hidden.
- Spawn menu (F1 / Q) and context menu (C).
- Physgun grabs and throws Dying Light's zombies; thrown zombies hit walls and
  land; no snapping back after release.
- Garry's Mod weapons damage and kill Dying Light's zombies; skeleton-fitted
  hitboxes and headshots.
- Rockets explode, grenades bounce and crossbow bolts stick on Dying Light's
  world; bullet impacts spark on walls.
- Weapons lit like Dying Light's scene (day/night), sway and bob with your
  movement.
- Garry's Mod's frame re-projected to Dying Light's camera with prediction and
  depth, 60 fps cap with lockstep for even pacing.
